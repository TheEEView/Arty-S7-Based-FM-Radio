MCP33131 Frame Timing

What the driver actually puts on the wire for one conversion — and what a 100 kHz sine from the mixer looks like coming back through it.

The driver is a three-state machine clocked at 60 MHz. Every frame is the same length regardless of the data: IDLE for one cycle, CONVST for one cycle, then READ_DATA for 31 cycles — 33 cycles, 550 ns, repeating forever with no gap. There is no start signal and no throttle; the machine falls straight from IDLE back into the next conversion.

Everything below is read off that structure, so the diagrams are exact cycle counts rather than sketches.

01One acquisition frame

The single-cycle CONVST pulse and the first SCLK rising edge are produced by the same clock edge, so the serial burst starts the instant the conversion is asked for.

02Where the bit gets captured

The capture happens in the sclk_half = '0' branch — the same branch that drives SCLK high. Both are registered off the same edge, so the flip-flop latches the value before that edge: the bit launched by the previous falling edge, one 60 MHz cycle earlier.

03A sine coming in

With the MAX2606 as the local oscillator, what reaches the ADC is the mixer output, not RF. Take a 100 kHz tone at 0.8 of full scale as a stand-in. At 1.818 MSPS that is 18.2 samples per cycle, and the 16-bit two's-complement codes on o_adc_data trace it directly.

05Three things the diagram exposes

Nothing waits for the conversion

SCLK's first edge lands 16.7 ns after CONVST rises. The MCP33131 needs its conversion time before the result exists, and the driver never waits for it — there is no timer and no end-of-conversion check. The first bit sampled is whatever SDO happens to hold at that moment.

1.818 MSPS against a 500 kSPS part

The schematic BOM calls out MCP33131-05-E/MS, the 500 kSPS grade. The frame rate here is about 3.6× that. Either the frame needs idle cycles added to slow it down, or the design wants the 1 MSPS grade — and even that is short of 1.818.

The capture edge has no margin to give

Fig. 2's 16.7 ns has to cover the ADC's SDO delay, the board round trip and the FPGA's setup time. Sampling on the falling edge instead is a one-line change and buys the other half of the SCLK period.

Minor: the file and entity are spelled mpc33131, the part is MCP33131. Worth fixing before the name spreads into constraints and testbenches.
